## Privacy Policy

This site is a documentation site for the open-source [Vincle](https://github.com/cjean-fr/vincle) project.

### Data collection

This site does not collect any personal data. It does not use cookies, tracking scripts, or third-party analytics.

### Local storage

The site stores your theme preference (automatic, light, or dark) and code-tab preferences in `localStorage`. This data never leaves your browser.

### Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Fontshare and Google Fonts. These providers receive the usual technical request information, including your IP address. Documentation pages may also display badges hosted by external providers.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cjean-fr/vincle).
